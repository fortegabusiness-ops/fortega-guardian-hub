## Must be completed before any expansion publication

1. Verify source relevance, exact excerpts, historical/current status and municipality boundaries for each accepted candidate.
2. Convert sufficient evidence into substantive service-specific local content, with scoped recommendations rather than invented conditions or customer experiences.
3. Recheck full rendered editorial bodies as well as local-only similarities. The existing 91 repeated-body groups cannot be cleared by extra keywords or shared FAQs.
4. For unsupported combinations, obtain additional defensible evidence or an owner-approved consolidation/non-publication decision. A finite search pass cannot prove that every possible source has been found or that 1,800 distinct pages can be justified.
5. Keep customer identities, passwords, access codes, sensitive layouts and site vulnerabilities out of public content and research artifacts.
6. Resolve regulated guarding eligibility through an appropriate qualified adviser/provider. Fortega states it has no licences; researching municipal guard duties does not establish Fortega's permission to deliver them. All 225 guard drafts remain blocked privately.
7. Confirm project delivery facts privately where needed; do not introduce public availability wording or imply confirmed coverage.
8. Verify the full backup. The existing archive covers source only, not the full database or storage. Cloud database export requires the owner's action in **More → Database → Manage → Settings → Export project data → Export data → Database → Export → Start export**. Storage files must be downloaded separately from **More → Storage**. Daily database backups and exports do not include storage files. No full dump script or service-role storage copier should be written. Three external image originals remain unresolved.
9. Re-run the 1,800-address, FAQ/schema, indexing, internal-link, deleted-URL, sitemap and content audits after approved rewrites. Current passing checks apply to the current drafts, not hypothetical future published pages.
10. Obtain separate explicit publication authorization. Only approved pages may enter production eligibility and sitemaps; research records never grant that eligibility.
