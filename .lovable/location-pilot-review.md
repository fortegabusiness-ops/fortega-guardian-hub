## Existing template claims to exclude from these drafts
- Alarm response “typically within seconds” in a named community.
- Every service delivered by licensed local technicians.
- “Local presence” without confirmed local staffing.
- A town-specific LocalBusiness identity or postal address implying a branch that has not been confirmed.
- Unqualified licensing assurances and universal coverage claims.
