## Complete

- Semrush Canadian keyword map covers all eight services. Demand and difficulty are point-in-time estimates for exact national queries, not town-level volume or ranking guarantees. Irrelevant suggested queries were excluded.

- Wrote 24 review drafts: eight service topics for Dawson City, Chetwynd and Happy Valley-Goose Bay. Review routes are noindex/nofollow, excluded from sitemaps and isolated from production eligibility.
- Fifteen drafts have separately written source-backed local planning sections (CCTV, access, intrusion, remote monitoring and automation in each town). Sources support conditional assessment questions, not site hazards or Fortega coverage.
- Six consulting/cyber drafts have insufficient local evidence and should be consolidated or enriched before promotion. Three guarding drafts are delivery-blocked.

## Difference check and limitations
Normalize city/province names, remove URLs, then compare five-word shingles, three-word shingles and token overlap. Compare local body sections alone; exclude shared service answers, checklists, navigation and delivery disclosures. The first 15 local bodies yielded no exact name-swap duplicates across the three towns. That is a limited lexical comparison, not semantic certification or a Google spam-safety threshold. These drafts share substantial service guidance. A source-backed local paragraph does not by itself justify a separate indexed page; review whether each page provides enough distinct value.
